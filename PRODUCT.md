## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML + Tailwind (CDN) + CSS propio + vanilla JS. Each page must be self-contained (CSS and JS inline) because Backbone AI previews serve external .css/.js as text/plain with nosniff. Delivered as views (`.blade.php`) via Import HTML into a new Backbone AI project "Agua Siembra" after local review.

## Users

- Households in Bogotá who buy bottled water and run out of it; they want it delivered on a schedule without thinking about it.
- Offices and restaurants (HORECA) that consume many 20 L jugs per month.
- Secondary: press, partners and people who care about the reforestation story.

## Product Purpose

Agua Siembra sells natural mineral water from the páramo (Guasca, Cundinamarca, 2.900 m) in returnable formats, and plants native trees with what it sells (1 tree per 10 jugs). The site must turn the brand's strong story into a working subscription: choose zone → consumption → plan → frequency → extras → summary → active subscription. Today the home promises a subscription but the plans page is a 404 and "Suscríbete" opens a Google Form.

## Positioning

Nobody in Colombia offers origin mineral water in returnable jugs with a self-managed digital subscription and visible impact tracking (trees planted, plastic bottles avoided). Real páramo spring water, not "manantial" in name only.

## Operating Context

- Coverage: Bogotá only. Each zone has a fixed route day; leave empties outside before 7 a.m., the same truck collects them.

- No testimonials, no subscriber numbers, no real zone data: do not fabricate.

## Product Principles

1. Sell the calm of never running out, and the forest that grows while it happens.
2. Show the price and the route day before asking for anything.
3. Impact is measured per subscriber, never vague.
4. The container comes back; nothing in the flow should imply waste.
